# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (Summary)

* **Our Pledge**: We commit to providing a welcoming, safe, and equitable environment for everyone, respecting all individuals regardless of background or status.
* **Our Standards**: Community members are expected to show empathy, kindness, and respect, while unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, non-consensual sharing of private info, and other inappropriate conduct.
* **Scope**: Applies within all community spaces and when officially representing the community in public spaces (e.g., via official emails, social media, or events).
* **Enforcement**: Report abusive or unacceptable behavior to **conduct@example.org** for a prompt, fair, and confidential investigation.
* **Consequences (Guidelines)**: Ranging from private corrections and formal warnings to temporary or permanent bans following the **Micro-Finance SVN Enforcement Policy**.
* **Attribution**: Adapted from the Contributor Covenant at [https://www.contributor-covenant.org](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
